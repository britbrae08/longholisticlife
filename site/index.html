Questions, answered

Frequently Asked Questions

You can begin without pressure, obligation, or having everything figured out.

Is the NEW CREATION guide really free?

Yes. It is a free digital resource from Long Holistic Life with no purchase required.

Is this another diet or exercise plan?

No. NEW CREATION is a whole-person framework. It helps you consider nourishment, energy, rest, stress, movement, relationships, outlook, environment, faith, and sustainable rhythms together.

What if I have tried many wellness plans before?

This guide does not ask you to start over or prove your discipline. It helps you learn from what has not worked, choose a more realistic starting point, and build from there.

Do I need to be in a certain place spiritually?

No. The guide is openly Christian and grounded in grace, stewardship, and trust in God, but you do not need to have everything figured out to benefit from it.

What if I have a medical condition or take medication?

Continue working with your licensed healthcare professional. The guide provides general education and reflection; it does not diagnose, treat, or replace individualized medical advice.

Will downloading the guide obligate me to purchase coaching?

No. Start with the guide, use what serves you, and decide later whether additional support feels right.
