Nourishment

Feed the woman who keeps everyone else going.

Nourishment is support—not another standard you have to meet perfectly.

When the day belongs to everyone else, it is easy to wait until you are depleted and urgently hungry before deciding what to eat.

Build simple meals around protein, fiber-rich plants, nourishing fats, water, and enough food to feel satisfied. Small, repeatable upgrades are more useful than another extreme reset.

Why this matters+
  • Steadier nourishment can support energy, focus, mood, and more intentional choices.
  • Planning one reliable option before hunger becomes urgent reduces the burden on willpower.
  • Caring for your body is part of caring for the life and people God has entrusted to you.
A question to notice+

Where do you most often become overly hungry, rushed, or under-fueled—morning, workday, afternoon, or evening?

Your next faithful step

Choose one meal you eat regularly. Add one supportive element—protein, produce, water, or enough food to leave satisfied.
