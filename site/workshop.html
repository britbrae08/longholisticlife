Questions before you join?

The Things Women Usually Wonder About

“What if my health situation feels complicated?”

That is exactly why we begin by prioritizing. You do not need to arrive knowing what is wrong or having a perfect goal. The workshop helps you sort through the noise and identify a useful place to begin.

“What if I already know a lot about health?”

Knowledge is useful, but implementation is often the missing piece. NEW CREATION helps you decide which information matters for your current season and translate it into a realistic rhythm.

“What if I cannot do everything?”

You will not be asked to. The workshop is built around choosing what matters most now and creating a Minimum Rhythm for difficult weeks.

“Will I be able to ask Brittany questions?”

Yes. The session is intentionally interactive. You can ask practical questions and receive coaching-oriented guidance within the educational scope of the workshop.

“Is this medical treatment?”

No. The workshop provides education, lifestyle reflection, practical coaching support, and accountability tools. It does not diagnose, prescribe, provide psychotherapy, or replace care from a qualified healthcare professional.
