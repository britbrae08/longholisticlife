QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU JOIN?

Common Questions, Answered

What if my health situation feels complicated?

You do not need to arrive knowing exactly what is wrong or what your goal should be. The workshop begins by helping you notice patterns and prioritize. If a concern requires medical evaluation or treatment, that remains with an appropriately licensed healthcare professional.

What if I already know a lot about health?

That may be exactly why this is useful. The workshop is less about collecting information and more about deciding what matters now, applying it to your current season, and choosing a realistic rhythm.

What if I cannot do everything?

You will not be asked to. NEW CREATION is designed to help you prioritize one useful next step and create a Minimum Rhythm for the weeks when capacity is lower.

Will I actually be able to ask Brittany questions?

Yes. This is an interactive Zoom workshop, and questions are encouraged. Brittany can offer education, coaching perspective, and practical implementation support within her scope.

Is the workshop medical treatment?

No. The workshop provides education, lifestyle reflection, practical coaching support, and accountability tools. It does not diagnose, prescribe, provide psychotherapy, or replace individualized medical or mental-health care.
